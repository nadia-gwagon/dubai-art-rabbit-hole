# dubai art rabbit hole

I've actually met Benjamin Saphiro. I went to his studio when my bf went to buy one of his paintings. We had to agree the price before he'd even show it to us. I didn't get it then. I kind of do now.

I remember he had a life-sized robot hanged in his studio. Not hanging, hanged 😱 And some portraits of ballerinas.

I'm writing down everything I know.

## note 1

He doesn't put them where you'll see them. He puts them where you'll stumble.

## note 2

There's a painter who has spent twenty years hiding his work instead of showing it. Back alleys in Hong Kong. A stairwell in London. Stickers that fade to white in a few weeks, so you either saw them or you didn't.
